Where the numbers come from

Every figure here is from an Indian government website, except the China time difference, which is from an Indian site.

  • Weather and 7-day forecast: India Meteorological Department local weather reports for Jaipur (station 42348) and Nagaur (station 42242). A web page cannot fetch IMD itself, so the figures are read from the two IMD pages and saved for it. Each sheet says when its copy was read.
  • Jaipur climate normals: IMD climatological table, 1991 to 2020.
  • Nagaur climate: Central Ground Water Board district report (2013) and the Nagaur district profile on the NIC-hosted district court site. IMD publishes no climate normals for the Nagaur station.
  • Indian Standard Time is kept by CSIR-National Physical Laboratory, New Delhi.
  • Abu Dhabi: the Embassy of India, Abu Dhabi gives UAE time as GMT+4 and IST as UAE time plus 1½ hours.
  • China keeps one time for the whole country, GMT+8, which is 2 h 30 min ahead of IST (clicktime.in).

The seconds tick on this device's own clock. NPL sends out Indian Standard Time over NTP servers, which a web page cannot read, so the page takes the time from your phone and applies the three official offsets. None of the three places changes its clocks during the year.
