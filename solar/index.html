Khul raha hai… Opening…

Yeh page sirf unhi devices par khulta hai jinhe owner ne approve kiya hai. This page opens only on devices its owner has approved.

Step 1

Access maangein. Yeh request us vyakti ko bhejein jisne aapko link diya hai. Ask for access. Send this request to the person who gave you the link.

Step 2

Apni key se kholein. Jo link woh wapas bhejein use dabayein, ya unka message yahan paste karein. Open with your key. Tap the link they send back, or paste their message here.

Dono steps ke liye wahi phone aur wahi browser istemal karein. Use the same phone and the same browser for both steps.
